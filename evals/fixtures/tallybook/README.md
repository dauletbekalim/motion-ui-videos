# Tallybook

Shared expenses for people who live together. Everyone adds what they paid,
Tallybook keeps a running balance, and settling up is one tap.

- **Add an expense in two taps**: amount, who paid, split evenly or by share.
- **Live balance**: everyone sees who owes whom, updated the moment anything changes.
- **Settle up**: one tap sends the exact amount through your bank app and clears the balance.
- **Recurring bills**: rent, internet and streaming split themselves every month.

Tagline: "no more awkward money talks."
Website: tallybook.example (waitlist). Platforms: iOS, Android, web.
Brand: warm off-white background, ink text, one accent green (#1f9d6a), rounded, friendly.
Font: "Plus Jakarta Sans" for UI, "IBM Plex Mono" for amounts.
